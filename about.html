About Us

Our story of identity, purpose, and women-led transformation.

History & Cultural Purpose

Founded under the leadership of Chief Mrs. Violet Uche Obiora (Onaedo), OUIPWF emerged to strengthen sisterhood among Igbo women, preserve heritage, and create structures for welfare, peace, and empowerment. As an affiliate of Unique Diamond Women Association, the foundation blends cultural pride with social action.

Our values are peace, unity, dignity, accountability, and compassion. We are committed to mentoring young girls, supporting families in need, and advancing women through knowledge, visibility, and community support.

Why We Exist

We exist because many women still need platforms of voice, dignity, and opportunity. OUIPWF is a home where culture is honored, pain is met with solidarity, and hope is turned into practical help—one family, one child, and one community at a time.
