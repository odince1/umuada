About the Foundation

ODIMMA UMUADA IGBO PRESTIGIOUS WOMEN FOUNDATION is an affiliate of Unique Diamond Women Association. We are committed to peace, unity, women empowerment, cultural continuity, and charitable service across communities.

Our Vision

To build a united and culturally rooted community where Igbo women lead with excellence, compassion, and dignity.

Our Mission

  • Promote and preserve Igbo cultural values.
  • Empower women through education, enterprise, and advocacy.
  • Support vulnerable families with practical care and hope.
  • Encourage peace, mentorship, and responsible leadership.
